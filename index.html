Example statement24 Sept 2026

Asking for a deadline extension politely

Here is a statement you can adapt when you need more time on a piece of work:

Good morning. I'm writing to ask for a short extension on the report due Friday. I've completed most of it, but I need two more days to check the figures properly. Would Monday afternoon work for you? Thank you for considering this.

Keep it short, say what is already done, give a clear new date, and end with a question so the other person can reply easily.

If the person says no, thank them and ask which parts matter most by the original date. That usually gets you a smaller, doable target.

Clarification21 Sept 2026

Receipt vs. invoice: what's the difference?

An invoice is a request for payment. You send it before the money arrives, and it lists what was supplied, the amount due, and when it must be paid.

A receipt is proof that payment was made. You give it after the money arrives, and it confirms the amount, the date, and how it was paid.

For a small shop, the usual order is: invoice first if the customer pays later, receipt once they pay. If the customer pays on the spot, a receipt alone is enough.

Keep copies of both. They are what you'll need if a customer disputes a payment or when you check your records at the end of the month.

Example statement18 Sept 2026

Replying to a customer who received the wrong item

Hello, I'm sorry you received the wrong item. Please keep it for now. I'll send the correct one today and arrange collection of the other at no cost to you. Thank you for letting me know.

Apologise once, say exactly what you will do, and say when. Avoid long explanations of how the mistake happened.

Clarification15 Sept 2026

What "due date" means on a job

A due date is the day the finished work must be delivered, not the day you start. If a job is due on the 30th, the finished work should be with the client by the end of that day unless a specific time is given.

When a time isn't stated, ask. "End of day" can mean 5 pm to one person and midnight to another.
